Key finding —

Sales-channel mix is the dominant driver of operating-margin variance, not region. Outlet carries a −14.0pp margin gap vs. company average (avg. discount ≈28% vs. ≈6% in-store) and Online a −2.0pp gap, while regional spread is under ±0.4pp. Shifting revenue mix toward In-store/Online and tightening Outlet discounting is the highest-leverage lever for margin.
